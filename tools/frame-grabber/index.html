    Storyboard

    Evenly spaced frames from the whole clip, on one sheet.

    01 Opened, never uploaded

    The video is read from disk through a local object URL. No request carries the file out of the tab, and the counter at the bottom of this page shows how many network requests this page has actually made.

    02 Pixels, at native size

    Each frame is drawn into a canvas at the video’s own resolution — 1920×1080 stays 1920×1080. PNG is lossless, WebP re-encodes at the quality you set, and nothing is ever scaled up.

    03 Sample times you can check

    Storyboard cells are the centres of equal slices of the clip, labelled with their real timecode. Frame numbers follow the frame rate above, so ±1 frame lands where you expect.

    Note. Frame accuracy depends on your browser and the codec inside the file: a phone clip recorded at a variable frame rate can drift from the number you set, so type the real rate into the frame-rate field if stepping looks off. A storyboard is a still image of the clip, not a video — keep the original file.
